About

I am a hands-on Staff Software Engineer and Engineering Manager with over 14 years of experience delivering software across mobile platforms, operating systems, and cloud backends.

At Google, I work on the Android Context Engine team building Pixel Magic Cue and Proactive Assistance - using AI models to anticipate user intent and surface real-time, low-latency help across apps. Previously at Google, I managed the Android Overview (Launcher app switcher) and Physical Keyboard/Trackpad teams, and worked as a C++ Tech Lead on ChromeOS System UI.

Before Google, I spent four years at Moonpig leading platform migrations to AWS serverless and rebuilding their mobile engineering division, following seven years delivering client systems at IBM iX.

I enjoy any technical challenge but I particularly enjoy building highly functional and engaging front end user experiences.
